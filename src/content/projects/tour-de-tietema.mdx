Tour de Tietema set out to disrupt the cycling world, making it more accessible, inclusive, and connected. Their identity challenged the unwritten rules of the sport.

Together with Jasper Bunschoten, I shaped the original visual identity and developed a wide range of assets — from the logo and brand guidelines to social media templates, merchandise, and even custom bike designs.
