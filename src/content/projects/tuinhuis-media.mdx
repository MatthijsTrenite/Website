Tuinhuis Media is a collective of experienced editors, writers, and journalists, and a creative hub for bold new talent. Their mission: to deepen public debate and foster greater understanding of the world and the people in it. The collective was born in a small garden house ("tuinhuis") in Amsterdam, a name and origin story that inspired the visual identity.

Tuinhuis Media distinguishes itself through content-first storytelling, with productions ranging from podcasts and TV shows to live events. Their work is sharp, relevant, and always in tune with the spirit of the times, whether it's a conversation at the office or a discussion at the bar. I created a visual identity that brings their voice to life, both online and offline.
